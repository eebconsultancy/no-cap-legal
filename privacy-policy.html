Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how EEB Consultancy LLC ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with the No Cap - Who Was Right? mobile application ("No Cap," "the App"). It describes what you submit to the App, what we send to our backend and to Google's Gemini API to generate a response, what we store and for how long, and the choices available to you.

By using No Cap, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Information You Submit

When you create a case in No Cap, you may enter:

  • A relationship label describing your connection to the other person (e.g. "Roommate," "Friend")
  • Your name and the other person's name, as you choose to enter them
  • A written description of the situation ("the situation")
  • Optional text evidence — messages, notes, or other text you paste or type in yourself
  • Answers to any clarifying questions the App may ask to generate a more accurate result

No Cap's evidence field accepts plain text only. The App does not currently offer a way to attach photos, screenshots, or images as evidence, and this Policy does not describe any such capability because it does not exist.

2. How This Information Is Used and Where It Goes

The case information described above is sent from your device, over an encrypted HTTPS connection, to our own backend service, which we run on Cloudflare's Workers platform. Our backend is dedicated to No Cap and is not shared with any other application.

Our backend forwards the relevant case content (relationship, names, situation, evidence text, and any clarification questions/answers, and — for the "Help Me Respond" feature — the previously generated analysis) to the Google Gemini API, solely for the purpose of generating the AI-based analysis or suggested response you requested. We do not send this content to Google for any other purpose, and we do not send it to any advertiser or data broker.

We do not control, and cannot make representations about, how Google or Cloudflare internally process or retain data on their own systems. We do not claim any specific retention period for either provider, and you should refer to Google's own privacy policy and Cloudflare's own privacy policy for information about their respective data practices.

3. What Our Backend Stores — and Does Not Store

Our backend does not persist your case content. The situation you describe, your evidence text, the names you enter, clarification answers, and the AI-generated analysis pass through our backend only for the duration of the request needed to generate a response. Our backend does not write this content to a database, file, or any other durable storage on our side.

4. Network and Abuse-Prevention Data

Cloudflare, as the infrastructure provider hosting our backend, may process request and network metadata — including IP addresses — as part of operating its network. This is inherent to how any HTTPS request reaches our backend and is outside the data our own application code stores.

Within our own backend code, specifically:

  • We derive a salted SHA-256 hash of your IP address (combined with a private, backend-only salt value) to identify abusive or excessive request patterns and to enforce fair-use limits on how many AI requests can be made from the same origin over a given period.
  • Our application does not store your raw IP address. Only the salted hash described above is written to our storage — never the IP address itself.
  • Rate-limiting and fair-use counters keyed to that hash automatically expire according to their configured time-to-live: short-term abuse-protection counters expire within a matter of minutes past their measurement window, and longer fair-use counters (used to bound daily, weekly, and monthly AI usage per origin) expire within a similar margin past their respective windows. None of these counters are kept indefinitely, and none of them contain your case content, name, or any other identifying information beyond the salted hash itself.
  • We also keep aggregate, non-identifying operational metrics (such as the total number of AI requests, successes, and failures across all users on a given day) to understand and manage service load and cost. These aggregate metrics are not tied to any individual user or hash, and are retained for up to 31 days before automatically expiring.

5. Information Stored On Your Device

By default, a case you create in No Cap exists only in the App's memory for that session and is not written to your device's storage. If you leave the App or it is closed, an unsaved case is not retained.

If you choose to tap "Save Case," — an explicit, opt-in action — the full case (including the situation, evidence, names, the AI-generated analysis, and any clarification answers) is written to a file on your device, stored in the App's private application data area at Application Support/NoCap/saved_cases.json. This location is private to the App and is not browsable through the Files app.

On a best-effort basis, we mark this saved-case file as excluded from device backups (such as iCloud or iTunes/Finder backups). This is a privacy precaution, not an absolute guarantee, and may not succeed in every circumstance.

We do not sync saved cases to any cloud service, and we do not have access to this file — it never leaves your device unless you separately choose to back up or transfer your device.

You can delete an individual saved case at any time from the History tab (press and hold a case, then choose "Delete"). This permanently removes that case from your device. There is currently no single "clear all data" control; each case is deleted individually.

6. Sharing a Verdict

If you choose to use the "Share Verdict" feature, No Cap opens your device's native share sheet with a plain-text summary (relationship, names, the decision, responsibility breakdown, summary, reasoning, mistakes, and recommendation). This summary deliberately excludes your original situation narrative, raw evidence, and clarification answers. Nothing is shared, sent, or transmitted anywhere unless and until you personally invoke the share sheet and choose a destination — the App does not share this content on its own or in the background.

7. Accounts and Sign-In

No Cap does not require or offer account creation, sign-in, or authentication of any kind. We do not collect your email address, password, or any other account credential in connection with using the App itself. (We may receive an email address you send to our support address voluntarily — see "Contact Us" below.)

8. Advertising, Analytics, and Tracking

No Cap does not use:

  • Advertising or ad networks of any kind
  • Third-party analytics services
  • Cross-app or cross-site tracking technologies
  • The Advertising Identifier (IDFA) or Identifier for Vendors (IDFV)
  • Cookies (No Cap is a native app, not a website, and has no cookie mechanism)

We also do not collect your location, access your contacts, or register a push-notification token. If any of this changes in a future version of the App, we will update this Policy and, where required by applicable law or App Store guidelines, seek your consent first.

9. Subscriptions and Payment

No Cap offers optional auto-renewable subscriptions, processed entirely through Apple's In-App Purchase system (StoreKit). When you subscribe, Apple handles the transaction directly — we do not receive, process, or store your payment card number, billing address, or other payment credentials. We only receive confirmation from Apple's systems that a valid subscription entitlement exists, which we use to unlock premium features within the App.

10. What You Should Avoid Submitting

No Cap is designed to help you reason through everyday interpersonal disputes. Please avoid entering more sensitive personal information than is necessary to describe your situation — for example, avoid including Social Security numbers, financial account numbers, government identification numbers, health/medical record details, or other similarly sensitive data in the situation or evidence fields, unless it is truly necessary and unavoidable to describe your dispute.

11. AI-Generated Content — Accuracy Limitations

No Cap's analysis, suggested responses, and any other AI-generated content are produced by a third-party large language model (Google's Gemini) based solely on the information you provide. This output can be inaccurate, incomplete, or reflect an incorrect understanding of your situation. It is provided for informational and entertainment purposes only and should not be relied upon as professional legal, medical, psychological, financial, or other professional advice. You should not make important decisions based solely on this content, and you should consult a qualified professional for guidance specific to your circumstances.

12. Children's Privacy

No Cap is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under the age required by applicable law in your jurisdiction. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us using the information below so we can address it.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as the App evolves. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date above and, where appropriate, provide additional notice within the App.
