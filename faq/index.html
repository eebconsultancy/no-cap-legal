Help & FAQ

Common questions about No Cap - Who Was Right?

What is No Cap?

No Cap is an informational, AI-powered app that helps you think through everyday interpersonal disputes. You describe a situation involving you and another person, and No Cap uses an AI model (Google's Gemini) to offer a perspective on it, including a summary, a responsibility breakdown, reasoning, possible mistakes on each side, and a suggested way to resolve things.

How does a verdict work?

You enter your relationship to the other person, both names, a description of the situation, and optionally some text evidence (like pasted messages). No Cap sends this information to its backend, which forwards it to Google's Gemini API to generate the analysis. If key information is missing, No Cap may ask up to one round of clarifying questions before producing a result.

Is the output professional advice?

No. No Cap's output — including any "Legal Perspective" or "Psychological Perspective" it may show — is general, AI-generated informational content only. It is not legal, medical, psychological, mental-health, or financial advice, and using No Cap does not create any professional relationship (such as an attorney-client or therapist-client relationship) between you and us. Consult a qualified, licensed professional when your situation calls for it.

Can AI output be wrong?

Yes. No Cap's output is generated by an AI model based solely on what you provide — it does not independently verify facts and does not hear from anyone else involved in your situation. It can be inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, misleading, or simply not fit your situation well. No Cap aims to weigh both sides based on what you share, but it cannot guarantee a neutral, accurate, complete, or fair result. Always independently evaluate the output, especially before making an important decision based on it.

What information should users provide?

Just enough to describe your situation clearly: your relationship to the other person, both names as you choose to enter them, what happened, and optionally any relevant text evidence such as pasted messages or notes. We recommend avoiding unnecessary sensitive personal information — for example, government ID numbers, financial account details, or detailed medical records — unless it's truly necessary to describe your dispute. See our Privacy Policy for more detail.

How are saved cases handled?

By default, a case only exists for your current session and isn't stored on your device. If you tap "Save Case," the full case is saved locally on your device only, at Application Support/NoCap/saved_cases.json — not in any account or cloud service, since No Cap doesn't have accounts. This file is excluded from device backups on a best-effort basis. You can delete an individual saved case at any time from the History tab.

What is No Cap Premium?

No Cap Premium is an optional, auto-renewable subscription that unlocks premium features in the app.

Weekly vs Monthly plan

No Cap offers a Weekly plan and a Monthly plan — there is no yearly plan. The actual price and billing period for each plan, in your local currency, are shown to you by Apple in the App Store before you confirm a purchase, and may vary by region.

Weekly free-trial eligibility

The Weekly plan may include a free introductory trial, but only when Apple's own systems determine you're eligible for it based on your Apple ID's subscription history. No Cap does not control or guarantee trial eligibility — it only shows trial messaging once Apple confirms you qualify. The Monthly plan does not include a promised free trial.

How to restore purchases

If you reinstall No Cap or switch to a new device, use the "Restore Purchases" option (available on the subscription screen) to re-link an existing, active subscription tied to your Apple ID.

How to manage or cancel an Apple subscription

No Cap does not process subscription cancellations or refunds directly. Manage or cancel your subscription at any time through your device's App Store account subscription settings — this is entirely handled by Apple, according to Apple's own subscription rules.
