Help & FAQ

Common questions about No Cap and how it works.

What is No Cap?

No Cap is an informational, AI-powered app that helps you think through everyday interpersonal disputes.

You describe a situation involving you and another person, and No Cap uses an AI model, Google's Gemini, to offer a perspective on it. Depending on the result and your access level, this may include a verdict, rightness percentages, a summary, reasoning, possible mistakes on each side, psychological insights, evidence-based insights, a legal perspective when relevant, and a suggested resolution.

How does a verdict work?

You enter your relationship to the other person, both names, a description of the situation, and optionally some text evidence, such as pasted messages or notes.

No Cap sends the relevant information through its backend to Google's Gemini API to generate the analysis.

If important information is missing or unclear, No Cap may ask one round of clarifying questions before producing the final verdict.

What does the free Quick Verdict include?

The free Quick Verdict gives you the core result without requiring a No Cap Premium subscription.

It includes the verdict result, rightness percentages for both sides, and a short summary of the outcome.

More detailed analysis is available with No Cap Premium.

What is No Cap Premium?

No Cap Premium is an optional auto-renewable subscription that unlocks the full verdict experience and other premium features.

Premium content may include:

  • Deeper reasoning about the dispute;
  • Key points from the analysis;
  • Possible mistakes made by each side;
  • Psychological perspective;
  • Evidence-based insights when applicable;
  • Legal perspective when relevant;
  • A suggested resolution; and
  • Help Me Respond.

Is the output professional advice?

No. No Cap's output — including any Legal Perspective or Psychological Perspective it may show — is general, AI-generated informational content only.

It is not legal, medical, psychological, mental-health, financial, or other professional advice, and using No Cap does not create a professional relationship, such as an attorney-client or therapist-client relationship, between you and EEB Consultancy LLC.

Consult an appropriately qualified and licensed professional when your situation calls for professional guidance.

Can AI output be wrong?

Yes. No Cap's output is generated by an AI model based on the information you provide.

No Cap does not independently verify the facts you submit and does not automatically hear from anyone else involved in your situation.

AI-generated output can be inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, misleading, inconsistent, or simply not fit your situation well.

No Cap aims to analyze the information you provide, but it cannot guarantee a neutral, accurate, complete, or fair result. Always independently evaluate the output, especially before making an important decision based on it.

What information should I provide?

Provide enough information to describe your situation clearly: your relationship to the other person, both names as you choose to enter them, what happened, and optionally any relevant text evidence such as pasted messages or notes.

We recommend avoiding unnecessary sensitive personal information, such as government identification numbers, financial account details, or detailed medical records.

See our Privacy Policy for more information about how submitted information is handled.

Can I upload screenshots or photos as evidence?

No. The current version of No Cap accepts text evidence only. You can type or paste relevant messages, notes, or other text into the evidence field.

No Cap does not currently provide a way to attach screenshots, photos, or other images as case evidence.

How are saved cases handled?

By default, a case only exists for your current session and is not stored persistently on your device.

If you tap "Save Case," the full case is saved locally in No Cap's private application data area on your device. No Cap does not upload saved cases to an account or to its own cloud storage.

No Cap does not have user accounts or cloud sync for saved cases.

The saved-case file is excluded from device backups on a best-effort basis. You can delete an individual saved case from the History tab.

Can I share a verdict?

Yes. Sharing is optional and uses Apple's native share sheet.

A free Quick Verdict share is limited to the free result information, such as the verdict result, rightness percentages, and a short summary.

If you have access to the full premium verdict, the shared text may also include additional verdict content available through the premium experience.

No Cap excludes the original situation narrative, raw evidence, clarification questions and answers, and internal case or backend identifiers from the generated share text.

What is Help Me Respond?

Help Me Respond is a No Cap Premium feature that uses the case and relevant verdict context to generate a suggested message you can use as a starting point when responding to the other person.

Suggested responses are AI-generated and should be reviewed and edited by you before sending. You remain responsible for any message you choose to send.

Weekly vs Monthly plan

No Cap offers a Weekly plan and a Monthly plan. There is currently no yearly plan.

The actual price and billing period for each plan, in your local currency, are displayed by Apple before you confirm a purchase and may vary by country or region.

Do the subscriptions renew automatically?

Yes. No Cap Premium subscriptions are auto-renewable subscriptions processed by Apple.

They continue according to Apple's subscription rules until canceled through your Apple account's subscription settings.

How do I restore purchases?

If you reinstall No Cap or use another device with the same Apple ID, use the "Restore Purchases" option on the subscription screen.

No Cap will ask Apple's systems to restore any applicable active subscription entitlement associated with your Apple ID.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

No Cap does not process subscription cancellations or refunds directly.

You can manage or cancel your subscription through your Apple account's subscription settings. Subscription billing, cancellation, and refund eligibility are handled by Apple according to Apple's applicable policies.

Does No Cap have accounts?

No. No Cap does not currently require or offer user accounts, sign-in, or cloud sync.

Does No Cap use ads or tracking?

No Cap does not currently use advertising, ad networks, third-party analytics services, or cross-app or cross-site tracking technologies.

For more information, see our Privacy Policy.
